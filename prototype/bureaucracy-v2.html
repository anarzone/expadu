Apply for your residence permit (§18a/b)
⏰ Due in 9 days — before your visa expires ✓ Verified 11 Jun 2026
▲

Apply at the Bezirksausländeramt Ehrenfeld — Cologne handles permits per district. The approval from the Bundesagentur für Arbeit happens internally between the offices; that's what makes it take weeks, and there's nothing you need to do for it.

How it goes
  1. 1
    Submit the online Erstantrag (form 33-F07) with your document scans.
  2. 2
    Wait for the appointment invitation — the BA approval runs internally meanwhile.
  3. 3
    Appear in person with the originals. Fingerprints are taken for the eAT card.
  4. 4
    Card arrives in ~4–6 weeks (≈ €100 first issue, paid at the office).

In individual cases further documents may be requested at the appointment — that line is Cologne's own, and it's the honest truth of German bureaucracy.

good to know
⛪ The religion box on the Anmeldung form

Declaring a recognized denomination enrolls you in church tax — 9% of your income tax in NRW, deducted from every salary. Leaving later costs a fee at the Amtsgericht. One honest checkbox decision saves four figures a year.

when you need it
✈️ Inviting family to visit?

Visa-required visitors need a Verpflichtungserklärung — a formal financial sponsorship you sign at the Ausländeramt (~€29). Takes one appointment; bring ID, proof of income and your Mietvertrag.
