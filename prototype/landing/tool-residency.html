When can you get permanent residency?

The Niederlassungserlaubnis ends visa renewals for good. Watch your timeline fill up.

2024
Blue Card: 21 months with B1, 27 with A1.
Skilled workers with a German degree: 2 years (§18c).

Date math is the easy part. The Ausländerbehörde also wants pension contributions, secured livelihood, B1 (track-dependent) and adequate housing.

Thresholds: §9 / §18c / §28 AufenthG, gesetze-im-internet.de · same engine as the Expadu app.
