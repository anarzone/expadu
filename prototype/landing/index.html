Before you ask

Questions, answered.

Is Expadu free?
Free during the Cologne launch. No card, no trial timer.
Which cities does it cover?
Cologne, properly — every place, office and tram line. The free tools work Germany-wide. Somewhere else? Leave your city in the footer and you’ll know the day we arrive.
Do I need to speak German?
No. The interface and every guide are in English. Germany will still send you letters in German — we tell you what each step means before you’re standing at the counter.
Is the advice official?
Every fee, deadline and document list links to its official source — Stadt Köln, BAMF, ELSTER — with the date we last verified it. If something changes, you can flag it with one tap.
Where does my data live?
On EU servers in Germany. GDPR isn’t a checkbox for us — it’s home turf.
Is there an app?
Expadu is a web app you can install in two taps — iPhone and Android, straight from the browser. No app store detour.
