Is Expadu free?
Free during the Cologne launch. No card, no trial timer.
Which cities does it cover?
Cologne, properly. The free tools work Germany-wide — and you can leave your city in the footer for the day we arrive.
Do I need to speak German?
No. The interface and every guide are in English — we tell you what each German step means before you're at the counter.
Is the advice official?
Every fee and deadline links to its official source — Stadt Köln, BAMF, ELSTER — with the date we last verified it.
Is there an app?
It's a web app you install in two taps — iPhone and Android, straight from the browser.
