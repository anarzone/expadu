# Today as a daily city assistant

Latest owner direction:

> It is just another event page. I want you to design 30% event in my and rest will be bureaucracy, composer, departures and overall cologne asistant. Use orange, cyan coloring with mix of yellow, purple and of course be braver.

This correction takes precedence over the earlier palette exploration in [the source brief](source-brief.md).

## Content priority

Target roughly 70% practical assistance and 30% event discovery in visual emphasis and content area. Events remain useful and expressive, but Today serves the person's whole day.

The practical core includes the next commitment, its relevant journey, Composer, paperwork, city services, Saved and weather. Preserve direct routes into the wider connected sections.

- Everyday: the next planned journey and commitment lead; Composer and paperwork follow.
- Significant event: a compact city highlight can lead when there is no more urgent personal issue. Practical capabilities remain prominent, and the event appears once.
- Relevant disruption: the affected journey adapts within the main practical composition. Show scheduled and expected times, a clear alternative-route action and optional Composer help.

## Visual direction

Orange and cyan are the primary accents. Yellow identifies city utility and purple supports discovery or paperwork detail. Give practical content expressive treatment: a connected journey/agenda surface, a main Composer workspace and a layered document folder.

Keep a compact header and clear navigation. On phones, practical information comes before ordinary discovery. Use short labels and familiar symbols. Keep progress counts factual: sample EU Blue Card progress is 1 of 5; Address registration has only “Not started,” without an invented total.

These concepts use city-specific sample content; implementation should obtain city data from configuration and records.

## Interaction contract for review

- A journey opens route options directly. Its Ask action adds that journey's context to Composer while preserving an existing draft and conversation. Opening an action never submits a message.
- Composer is editable and understandable as an assistant request, with explicit sending. Quick actions offer a starting point rather than silently changing plans.
- A paperwork row opens that exact process. Additional processes remain available through All paperwork. Do not invent user-facing legal advice, requirements, fees or deadlines.
- Save keeps an event idea; Plan creates a proposal for explicit review. Confirmed agenda items remain distinguishable from proposals and saved ideas.
- Fixed events retain their supplied start and end times.
- Swipe left or double-tap reveals a compact red Delete action beside the still-visible activity. Reveal alone does not delete. Include keyboard access and undo after removal.
- Preserve scroll and focus when opening or closing contextual content. Use short, interruptible transitions; reduced motion keeps the same available states.
- Empty agenda offers an action. Unavailable data offers retry. Stale information shows its update time. Simulated status and illustrative media remain explicit.

## Review boundary

The six page views and interaction sheet are static visual proposals. They are not browser screenshots or a working interactive prototype. Browser behavior, responsive sizing, focus, contrast and reduced motion need validation during implementation after design review.

All event scenes, status examples, progress and weather are sample content. No live integration is implied.
