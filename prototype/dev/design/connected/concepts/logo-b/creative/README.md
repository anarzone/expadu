# Expadu — creative directions for the original ribbon

Three review directions following the owner's request for more creative change
and freedom to use any colours. All keep the original two-piece Concept B
ribbon. No application logo or shared page code has been changed.

## Directions

1. **Ember — coral / violet.** Recommended starting point. Keeps the original
   warmth in the upper ribbon and gives the lower piece a distinct violet tone.
2. **Tide — turquoise / deep teal.** Mint highlights with a cooler teal palette.
3. **Cherry — cherry / plum.** Bright cherry above a darker plum lower ribbon.

Each has internal fold planes, a stronger Geist Bold (700) wordmark and a closer
mark-to-wordmark pairing. The outer contours are copied from the minimal polish
of the original B, including its two separate pieces, upper cap and open cutouts.
The fold shading is drawn inside clipped contours. There is no new connected-E
silhouette, raster texture, blur, lighting filter or embedded font.

The dark-background variants lift the lower ribbon's tones for visibility.
Use the flat two-colour mark for small placements. The preview shows both the
folded and flat treatments, including flat marks at 16, 24 and 32px tall.

## Files in each direction

- `mark.svg`: folded mark, transparent, 32 × 40 viewBox.
- `mark-flat.svg`: flat two-colour mark with the same silhouette.
- `logo.svg`: folded mark and dark wordmark, 124 × 40 viewBox.
- `logo-dark.svg`: adjusted ribbon tones and white wordmark for dark backgrounds.
- `icon.svg`: app-icon study, 96 × 96 viewBox with an opaque square background.
- `png/`: ten exports, including transparent marks/logos and opaque app icons.

Mark PNGs are 32 × 40 and 256 × 320. Logos are 248 × 80 and 992 × 320.
App-icon studies are 128 × 128 and 512 × 512. The preview applies rounded
masks; exported app icons remain full squares.

The SVGs contain editable vector paths, gradients and clipping paths. Lettering
is outlined. All gradient/shape IDs are unique within each SVG. `palette.json`
records the light/dark colours used in each direction.

## Review and downloads

`preview.png` compares all three directions. `preview.html` is a responsive local
viewer with SVG download links; it uses existing project fonts and the original
logo in the parent folder. The portable ZIP includes all 15 SVGs, all 30 PNGs,
the PNG review board, palette data and this guide. It excludes the local viewer.

Checked at 1320px and 390px viewport widths: images and fonts loaded, with no
horizontal overflow. All exports were checked for correct dimensions, clear
cutouts, expected alpha, edge clearance, unique SVG IDs and unchanged outer
ribbon paths. The flat mark retains two separate connected pieces at 16px tall.
Original B assets remain unchanged.

These are creative proposals for review, not an approved replacement identity.
