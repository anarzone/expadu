What informed these examples

Borrow the principles.
Keep Expadu’s personality.

Public product screenshots, official design guidance and release articles. These are original Expadu explorations, not reproductions or official brand palettes. Publication dates are shown where relevant.

01

Remove the competing layers

The earlier preview put a cool selection inside a warm field, inside a different page colour. Here, header and page share one canvas; colour has a specific job.

02

Keep the task in focus

Linear’s 2026 refresh reduces visual noise and warns against overly warm neutrals. Airbnb’s Trips screens keep the interface quiet around useful content.

03

Make motion explain a change

Tabs move their selection; routes unfold; saving gets a short acknowledgement. No automatic carousels, looping trains or pretend live tracking.

Research informs the choices; it does not establish which one Expadu’s users prefer. All hex values and animation timings here are our proposals. Sample journeys are the existing 7 September 2026 capture, not live directions.
