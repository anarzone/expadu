Onboarding — v3 additions

Only two changes to the shipped 4-step flow. Both prevent wrong deadlines — they don't add a fifth step. Driving licence stays OUT of onboarding (it's a teaser card on the bureaucracy page).
① Step 2 addition — appears only after "Non-EU" is picked
How did you enter Germany?
Why we ask: this sets your real permit deadline — the 90-day clock and the visa-expiry clock are different animals.
② Step 3 addition — under the Veedel picker
Temporary housing (Airbnb, hotel, a friend's couch) can't be registered — we'll pause your Anmeldung deadline until you move in, so no false alarms.
