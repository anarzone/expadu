# Overture Maps contact facts — 23 Oct 2026

**What:** websites and phone numbers from Overture Maps Places (release `2026-10-21.0`) for places
matched one to one with an Overture place. Local `expadu` only; not on staging or production.

## Method

1. Overture places in Cologne's box (105,087), less `permanently_closed`, read with DuckDB from the public release.
2. Matched to active local places: ≤ 50 m, name similarity (Jaro–Winkler on names without generic words)
   ≥ 0.92, or ≥ 0.85 within 25 m, and a compatible kind (Overture taxonomy path ⊇ the kind's words).
3. Strictly one to one: an Overture place matching two local places, or a local place with two close
   candidates, is held, not merged. 2,843 matches.
4. Each match is recorded as a `place_fact_observations` row: provider `overture`, the Overture id,
   `contact` {website, phone} only. Maps, social, booking and delivery links are not a place's own
   website and are dropped (105).
5. `PlaceFacts` resolves contact field by field: the place's own source (OSM) decides where it lists a
   value; Overture only fills gaps. Where Overture says something else, OSM's value stays and the fact
   carries `disputed_by: ["overture"]` (Overture is often the stale side: old domains, a typo'd
   facebook link). A record silent on a field never erases it.
