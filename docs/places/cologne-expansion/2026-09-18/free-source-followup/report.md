# Free source follow-up — 18 September 2026

Owner constraint: all acquisition and media must be free. The paid pilot is cancelled, with zero expenditure. No live import or blanket rights approval occurred.

## Newly measured source: Panoramax

The public catalogue returned eight photographs explicitly linked from named Cologne destinations in OSM. All eight have CC BY-SA 4.0 metadata, licence URLs, producer credits, capture dates and original image URLs. Six destinations previously had no image candidates in the preparation package: Bona’me, Crêpes Lounge Rheinauhafen, Sattgrün, Novotel Koeln City, Achterdeck and Hellers Volksgarten. Ubierring Park and Severinstorburg already had other images.

The separate no-cost 300-place gap test completed without request errors. Nearby cameras pointing toward the requested location returned photos for 16 sample places: seven dining, two playgrounds, four sport/leisure and three culture. None of the 60 sampled parks returned a result within the narrow 3–30m search. These 16 are discovery leads, not confirmed depictions or a citywide coverage estimate. Building centroids, camera positions, vegetation, occlusion and old capture dates can all matter. This sample is geographically spread but not statistically representative.

The eight directly linked images also remain subject/quality unreviewed; Sattgrün’s image dates from 2019. No claim is made that street photos are suitable card images without inspection. The main catalogue counts stay unchanged; this supplement explicitly records six additional source-linked groups for review.

Sources: [Panoramax reuse terms](https://panoramax.fr/mentions-legales), [public catalogue](https://api.panoramax.xyz/api), [federated catalogue documentation](https://docs.panoramax.fr/federated-catalog/). Exact queries, raw responses and per-image rights are retained alongside this report.

## Richer tourism lead: Data Hub NRW / DISH

[Tourismus NRW](https://tourismusverband.nrw/themen/datenmanagement) confirms integration of DISH restaurant records. Its dashboard lists 1,487 dining records for Cologne plus Rhein-Erft; this is neither Cologne-only nor a photo count. Its public widget was inspected, and a 15-record statewide sample was saved: only four had an image field. That sample contains no Cologne rows and cannot estimate Cologne coverage. It proves that a broad open-data record count must not be mistaken for universal photo availability.

The [free feed onboarding](https://tourismusverband.nrw/themen/datenmanagement/open-data-finder) requests contact information and terms acceptance before issuing a key. No application was submitted and no other application's access token was reused. Cologne-only enumeration, source overlap, exclusion of municipal assets and per-photo licensing remain outstanding.

## Additional free photo repositories to measure

[Mapillary](https://help.mapillary.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001770409-CC-BY-SA-license-for-open-data) documents its imagery licence and attribution. Their Cologne destination-photo gains were not measured in this pass; neither is counted as an acquired source. Street imagery quality and relevance remain separate from licence eligibility.

The next source work should finish the NRW Cologne-only inventory and evaluate these free repositories and deeper Commons categories against missing dining/sport/playground records. Do not reinstate paid providers or municipal sources. Majority coverage is still unfulfilled.
