## Important exclusions and holds

- **Stadion:** the proposed destination is a shopping row at 50.94307, 6.95796. Its 90 stadium photos are false matches, including RheinEnergieSTADION and the Sporthochschule stadium.
- **Apple Store / Müller / Subway:** flowers, speaker Alexander Müller, and musician photographs are not store photos. Brand or surname matches are rejected.
- **Flora, AQUALAND, Innerer Grüngürtel, TimeRide, Stadtwald and Stammheimer Schlosspark:** other catalogue rows already illustrate the destination. Empty duplicate rows do not create new coverage.
- **Stadtgarten restaurant and Haus Balchem library:** an illustrated parent/building already exists. Separate business/facility scope needs resolution before claiming new destination coverage. These are excluded from the new count, not declared permanently identical.
- **Lentpark:** the suggested image is a construction site of the pool/ice facility, while the empty row is a park. An illustrated sporting-facility row already exists.
- **Rosengarten:** seven unrelated rows receive the same six photos. Shops and gardens in other parent parks are rejected; only the plausible Volksgarten garden is retained on a scope hold.
- **Starbucks / Burger King:** other branches are rejected. One Starbucks point is about 36 m from a Neumarkt building photograph, and one Burger King has Wiener Platz address support, but the captions describe a building/gallery rather than establishing the current stores. Both stay on additional holds.
- **Wildpark Dünnwald, Merheimer Heide, Leo-Amann-Park, Gereonsdriesch:** animals, a table-tennis table, murals and sculptures can be correctly located while still failing to depict the venue recognisably. These stay on additional holds.
- **Haus Unkelbach / Em Krützche / Zum Alten Brauhaus:** historic counter-cabinet details need visual recognisability and currentness checks. One Em Krützche file has a conflicting Schreckenskammer filename and is rejected. The northern Zum Alten Brauhaus branch is rejected because the photo identifies Severinsviertel.
- **Jugendpark / Orangerie / Weinmuseum / Tropenhaus:** administration building versus park, historic building versus theatre, museum versus dining category, and Urwaldhaus versus Tropenhaus remain unresolved identity or scope questions.
- **Westfriedhof:** cemetery entrance and avenue candidates survive; grave details, sculpture/signature details and the cadastral map are rejected.

No proposed asset was approved. The existing municipal-provenance flag and author/credit strings were checked; a municipal venue name in the description alone is not treated as municipal photographic provenance. Retained assets still require source-level rights verification and review of any depicted art or people. Existing media counts likewise do not imply that those assets are approved for production.
