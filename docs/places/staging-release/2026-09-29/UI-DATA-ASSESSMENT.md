# Places data and interface assessment — 29 September 2026

Refs EXP-69, EXP-70 and EXP-72. This records evidence and a recommendation; it does
not settle the separate Places-page decision or change the approved prototypes.

- **Photo evidence:** the latest verified staging audit is dated 24 September:
  87 selected approved, active photographed destinations out of 3,275 eligible
  destinations (2.6565%). Those 87 assets passed actual-byte validation, with no
  selected municipal-origin assets. The delivery added or repaired photos for
  53 additional unique destinations, taking the count from 34 to 87. This is not
  a refreshed 29 September or production coverage count. The new 4,643-record
  package adds no approved photos; the 75% objective remains unmet.
- **Rights:** only approved, healthy assets may be displayed, with attribution.
  Stadt Köln structured place data and place media remain excluded, including
  municipal records republished by another provider. A source lead is not a
  photo approval. See the [24 September photo report](../../cologne-expansion/2026-09-24/photo-delivery/REPORT.md).
- **Useful data:** the full staging rollback rehearsal verified 4,643 prepared
  records and 4,084 Composer identities. The remaining 559 stay outside automatic
  recommendations. This package covers 24 categories and 86 neighbourhoods,
  including cafés and restaurants. It has not been committed to staging or
  production. These package figures are not the entire city's unique-place count.
- **Limitations:** fees, access and hours often remain unknown. There are zero
  verified free/public football matches in this package. Preserve known negative
  facts, booking and age conditions, source freshness, destination/facility IDs,
  and the distinction between a source centre and a verified entrance. Do not
  invent descriptions, availability or ratings to make cards appear complete.
- **Assessment:** retain Places as shared data for Composer, events and journeys.
  A photo-optional interface is viable when useful activities, practical facts,
  neighbourhood context and next actions carry the experience. It should not
  depend on an image grid or become an undifferentiated directory. A dedicated
  page needs a clear discovery purpose to justify itself.
- **Decision status:** the connected design record says the owner reopened the
  separate Places-page direction on 28 September. Preserve the current prototype
  and keep it outside the consistency pass until that decision is made. The prior
  field-guide concept remains a reference, not a newer final approval. No UI
  implementation is part of this assessment.
