# Data map: the iPhone app and its server (16 Nov 2026)

One page for one lawyer hour (track, week 3). It covers what the iPhone app and its server keep about a person, why, on what legal basis, for how long, and who else sees it. It describes the code on `staging` as of 16 Nov 2026 (PRs up to #501). The live privacy policy (`resources/views/marketing/datenschutz.blade.php`) describes the web app. Its missing iPhone parts are drafted in `2026-11-16-app-privacy-section.md`.

**Legend:**
- Basis: (b) contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR); (a) consent (Art. 6(1)(a)); (f) legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
- "Pseudonym" means it is stored under `Pseudonym::for($user)`, a keyed hash, not the account id.

**Never kept or learned:**
- Health, religion or beliefs, sexuality, politics or a union. `Memory` refuses such facts in English and German.
- Health services, places of worship and LGBTQ+ venues, or events marked sensitive. `Sensitive` applies to signals and to trips kept for routines.
- Raw location for personalisation. The coordinates of Home or Work never go to Anthropic.

**Processors and transfers:**
- **Anthropic PBC (USA).** Processes under its DPA with standard contractual clauses and does not train on the data. Model: Claude Haiku.
- **Sentry.**
- **Hosting:** Hetzner (Germany) behind Cloudflare.
- **Routing:** self-hosted MOTIS; Transitous and TRIAS as configured.
- **Map tiles:** OpenFreeMap.
- **Email:** the configured mail provider.

**Rights:**
- **Export:** Download your data includes everything above that's kept, including `remembered`, `trips_kept_for_routines` and `routines`.
- **Erase:**
  - Personalisation off erases all of its rows, memory, trips and routines.
  - Routine learning off erases trips and routines.
  - Deleting the account erases everything.
- **Withdraw:** each consent is withdrawn where it was given (Settings; "What Expadu knows"; Composer's notice).
