# Expadu market evidence (go/no-go input)

Compiled 16 Nov 2026. Data from before 2025 is tagged **[older]**. Lines marked **own calc** are my arithmetic on the cited source. Lines marked **estimate** are my inference, not a published figure.

## 1. Market size

**Germany**
- Foreign nationals: 14,070,225 in the central register (AZR) on 31 Dec 2025, almost unchanged from 14,062,000 in 2024. EU citizens fell by 75k and non-EU rose by 83k; India grew by +34k. The population-register count is 12,454,493 (30 Nov 2025). Sources: [Destatis table](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Gesellschaft-Umwelt/Bevoelkerung/Migration-Integration/Tabellen/auslaendische-bevoelkerung-bundeslaender.html), [Destatis AZR, 2 Apr 2026](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Gesellschaft-Umwelt/Bevoelkerung/Migration-Integration/auslaenderstatistik-stichtag.html). Average stay is 14.8 years, so most foreign residents are long settled.
- Arrivals: about 1.48M people moved to Germany in 2025 (−13%). Net migration was 235k, down 45% from 430k ([Destatis PM 184, 1 Jun 2026](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2026/06/PD26_184_12411.html)).
- Skilled workers:
  - 471,000 non-EU nationals held a time-limited work residence permit at end-2025. India is the largest group with 62,000 ([Destatis, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Im-Fokus/Fachkraefte/Zuwanderung/_inhalt.html)).
  - 420,000 employees held work-based permits in June 2025, up from about 200,000 in 2020. About 164,000 employees hold a Blue Card ([Bundesagentur für Arbeit, 18 Feb 2026](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/presse/2026-07-fuenf-jahre-fachkraefteeinwanderungsgesetz)).
- Chancenkarte (opportunity card): 11,497 visas were issued between 1 Jun 2024 and 15 Jun 2025, 3,721 of them to Indians ([Bundestag Drs. 21/692](https://dserver.bundestag.de/btd/21/006/2100692.pdf)). The government says it does not know how many holders found work ([IW, 28 Nov 2025](https://www.iwkoeln.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Studien/Kurzberichte/PDF/2025/IW-Kurzbericht_2025-Chancenkarte.pdf)). A law firm reports that monthly Blue Card visas halved after Aug 2025 ([VisaGuard](https://www.visaguard.berlin/post/evaluation-fachkr%C3%A4fteeinwanderungsgesetz-zahl-der-eingewanderten-fachkr%C3%A4fte-bricht-deutlich-ein)); this is not official data.
- International students: 402,083 in WS 2024/25, 14% of all students, with a record 116,600 in their first year ([DAAD, Nov 2025](https://www.daad.de/en/press-releases/erneut-hohe-zahl-an-internationalen-studierenden-in-deutschland/)).

**NRW:** 3,265,705 foreign nationals in the AZR, 31 Dec 2025 ([Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Themen/Gesellschaft-Umwelt/Bevoelkerung/Migration-Integration/Tabellen/auslaendische-bevoelkerung-bundeslaender.html)).

**Cologne** ([KSN 3/2026](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/mediaasset/content/pdf15/statistik-einwohner-und-haushalte/ksn_3_2026_bev%C3%B6lkerung_in_k%C3%B6ln_2025.pdf), [KSN 8/2026](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/mediaasset/content/pdf15/statistik-einwohner-und-haushalte/ksn_8_2026_bevoelkerungsbewegungen_in_koeln_2025.pdf))
- Population and nationality, 31 Dec 2025:
  - 1,100,076 residents, of whom 237,750 are non-German (21.6%).
  - 473,731 residents (43.1%) have a migration background.
  - Largest groups: Türkiye 48,640, Italy 17,691, Ukraine 16,710, Bulgaria 10,017, Iraq 9,169.
- Moves from abroad in 2025: 16,356, down 16%. Net from abroad: +7,085.
- Non-German arrivals in 2025, counting moves from other German cities too: 24,866.
  - EU: 6,000. Türkiye: 2,528. Ukraine: 2,309. Syria: 1,302. Iran: 998. India: 937. Afghanistan: 916. China: 597. The Americas: 1,523.
- Students, WS 2025/26: 92,656 at Cologne's universities ([Statistical Yearbook 2025, Table 613](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/mediaasset/content/pdf15/statistisches_jahrbuch_koeln_2025_kap_6_bildung_und_ausbildung.pdf)). About 12,100 are non-German (own calc, summing the table). That includes Uni Köln 5,705 and TH Köln 2,626, and it also counts foreigners who went to school in Germany. Uni Bonn reports 5,285 international students ([uni-bonn.de](https://www.uni-bonn.de/en/studying), undated).

**English-speaking segment:** no official statistic exists. Estimate: Cologne gets roughly 4–10k new English-using arrivals a year, made up of skilled third-country nationals (India, Iran, China, the Americas, about 4k) plus part of the 6k EU movers and new international students. Much of the remaining in-migration is humanitarian (Ukraine, Syria, Afghanistan, about 4.5k), and those arrivals have different needs.

**Pain is real and measured**
- InterNations Expat Insider 2025 ([Germany page](https://assets.in-cdn.net/expat-insider/2025/germany-2025)):
  - Germany ranks 42nd of 46 overall and 43rd for Ease of Settling In.
  - It ranks last (46th) on Expat Essentials, the admin, digital, housing and language index, and has done so every year since 2022.
  - 63% of expats struggle to make local friends.
- InterNations 2026: Germany ranks 30th of 31 ([iamexpat, 24 Jul 2026](https://www.iamexpat.de/expat-info/germany-news/germany-2nd-worst-country-move-say-internationals)).
- **[older]** Cologne, 2024 city ranking: 48th of 53 overall, 51st on Expat Essentials, 48th on Admin and Digital Life, and last (53rd) on Language. 59% say it is hard to live there without German ([InterNations 2024](https://assets.in-cdn.net/expat-insider/2024/german-cities-40498)).
- Because of staff shortages, Cologne's immigration office stopped processing Mülheim cases at the Mülheim site from 1 Jan 2026 ([stadt-koeln.de](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/service/adressen/auslaenderamt)).

## 5. Willingness to pay

**What expats demonstrably pay for:** blocked accounts (€119–159 setup plus €9–9.90 a month), mandatory health insurance, housing fees, relocation tasks (about €300 each) and employer-paid relocation. All are compulsory or high-stakes; none is a daily-life convenience.

**RevenueCat State of Subscription Apps 2026** (19 Mar 2026; 115k apps, $16B revenue; [report](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026/), [travel](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-travel), [benchmarks](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/subscription-app-trends-benchmarks-2026))
- Share of downloads that pay by day 35:
  - Median across all apps: **2.0%**.
  - Hard paywall 10.7%, freemium 2.1%.
- Western Europe:
  - 2.0% pay by day 35 and 5.0% start a trial.
  - Trial-to-paid: 29.7%.
  - Lifetime value per paying user after one year (yearly plans): $26.64.
- Travel:
  - 4.1% of downloads start a trial, and 43.5% of trials convert.
  - **Lowest yearly price of any category (about $20)** and the **lowest monthly revenue a year after launch ($35 median, against about $72 for all apps)**.
- Only **17.3%** of new apps reach $1K monthly revenue within two years, and 4.6% reach $10K.
- About 72% of annual subscribers cancel in their first year.
- AI apps earn more per user in year one: $30.16 against $21.37.

**Illustration (own calc):** 10,000 Cologne downloads × 2% paying × about $25 a year ≈ **$5k a year**. A consumer subscription alone cannot fund the product at Cologne scale.

## 6. Regulation: real but small for SMEs

**GDPR fines** (own calc on CMS [enforcementtracker.com](https://www.enforcementtracker.com/) data, 3,280 fines with amounts, accessed 16 Nov 2026)
- The **median fine is €8,000**. 57% are €10k or less, 84.7% are €100k or less, and 94.6% are €1M or less.
- Since 2025: 772 fines, median €8,485, and 7.3% at €1M or more.
- Germany has only 117 published fines, median €16k.
- The most common reason is "insufficient legal basis" (964 fines).
- CMS's own 2026 report counts €6.11bn in total and names tracking and profiling as the cause of the *highest* fines ([CMS, 21 May 2026](https://cms.law/en/deu/publication/gdpr-enforcement-tracker-report/executive-summary)).
- DLA Piper: about €1.2bn in fines in 2025 and 443 breach notifications a day ([DLA Piper, Jan 2026](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/2026/01/dla-piper-gdpr-fines-and-data-breach-survey-january-2026)).

**Small AI company precedent:** Italy's regulator fined Luka Inc. (Replika) **€5M** on 10 Apr 2025. The reasons were no legal basis, poor transparency and no age checks ([EDPB](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/ai-the-italian-supervisory-authority-fines-company-behind-chatbot-replika_ga)).

**DPIA is mandatory:** the German regulators' must-list requires a data protection impact assessment for item 2, "extensive processing of… location", and item 7, "comprehensive profiles on the interests… or personality" ([DSK list](https://www.lda.bayern.de/media/dsfa_muss_liste_dsk_en.pdf)). Expadu's GPS tracking plus taste profile matches both.

**AI Act**
- Art. 50(1) has applied since **2 Aug 2026**: users must be clearly told they are talking to an AI unless that is obvious from context. The AI Omnibus (Reg. (EU) 2026/1744, in force 27 Jul 2026) did not delay it.
- Marking of AI-generated content: systems on the market before 2 Aug 2026 have until 2 Dec 2026.
- Fines go up to €15M or 3% of turnover ([Goodwin, Aug 2026](https://www.goodwinlaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/08/alerts-technology-dpc-eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-now-in-force)).
- A private recommender or assistant is not high-risk. My reading of Annex III(7) is that the migration category covers systems used *by or for public authorities*.
- *Implication:* Expadu's own rule of "no AI wording in the UI outside Settings" should be checked against Art. 50(1).

## 7. Synthesis

**Succeeds if…**
1. **It charges at a compulsory, high-stakes moment.** Use Paperwork as the wedge and earn partner revenue on blocked accounts, insurance and banking. Precedents: Fintiba/Expatrio's €42M, Feather's 100k customers, and a price rise that went through after 9 years.
2. **Someone other than the user pays.** Candidates are employers, universities, the city or transit (KVB/VRS) as sponsors. Precedents: Transit is profitable with 200 agency sponsors, Localyze was B2B, and Integreat is funded by municipalities.
3. **A frequent utility carries retention.** Live transport is a daily use. Transportation keeps 4.8% on day 30, against 3.1% for travel, so personalisation rides on that habit and is not the habit itself.
4. **Personalisation is visibly reliable and under the user's control.** Precedents: Gemini's "over-personalization", Siri's personal context as its weakest area, the slide from Google Now to Discover, and memory being "table stakes".
5. **Acquisition rides arrival funnels at near-zero cost.** The funnels are 12k non-German students in Cologne, 116,600 new international first-years nationwide, Indian skilled workers (62k, the fastest-growing group) and employers' onboarding. At $35 median monthly revenue after a year, the business cannot afford paid acquisition.

**Fails if…**
1. **Consumer subscription is the main revenue.** 2.0% of downloads pay by day 35, 3% of AI users pay at all, travel has the lowest prices, and 72% of annual subscribers cancel in year one.
2. **"Knows you" is the moat.** ChatGPT has 800–900M weekly users, and fewer than 10% of them try another assistant. Gemini Personal Intelligence and personal Siri in iOS 27 now read Mail and Messages on the owner's own iPhone 15 Pro. Personal-AI startups ended badly: Dot (24.5k downloads), Pi (absorbed by Microsoft), Humane and Rabbit.
3. **The niche shrinks and churns out by design.** Cologne's arrivals from abroad fell 16% in 2025, national net migration fell 45%, and Blue Card visas reportedly halved. Expats stop needing help once settled, with an average stay of 14.8 years.
4. **Free public or incumbent tools commoditise each feature.** For bureaucracy: Integreat with its AI chat, the Work-and-Stay-Agentur, and many cheap letter apps. For transit: KVB, DB and Google. For events in Cologne: Rausgegangen, now owned by DuMont.
5. **Accuracy and compliance costs outrun a solo founder.** Citymapper was the category leader and still lost £7.4M on £5.1M revenue. Wrong bureaucracy advice hurts trust, and Replika was fined €5M for basics: legal basis and age checks. Expadu also needs a DPIA and Art. 50 disclosure.

**Gaps:**
- No official count of English-speaking expats.
- No Chancenkarte total for all of 2025.
- No KVB app usage figures.
- No documented failures of German expat consumer apps beyond small projects.
