# The track: weeks 1–3, then the beta (from 16 Nov 2026)

The owner's verdict call on 16 Nov: continue, iPhone only, smart personalisation is the product (Composer is the engine that knows the person; Travel, Paperwork, events are its tools). Evidence: `2026-11-16` reports (Composer graded 3.27/5 blind, personalisation adds nothing measurable yet, 6 accounts in production, consumer money thin, payers are institutions). The Apple Developer account comes next month; the beta starts when TestFlight works.

**Every piece of work must move one of the goals below. Anything else waits.** At the end of each week I report against this page: done, not done, why.

## The decision we're working toward

Seven weeks after the beta starts:

1. **People keep using it**: at least 25% of testers weekly active after 4 weeks.
2. **The engine is good**: Composer graded blind at least 4.0/5 with at most 10% poor answers, and personalised answers preferred at least 60% of the time over the same answers without the person's picture.
3. **Someone would pay**: at least one organisation (international office, employer relocation team, the city, KVB/VRS) willing to pay for or pilot it.

Two or three pass: all in. None: stop, or keep Travel as a personal project.

## Frozen until the decision

- The web app (Inertia), the prototype mock, their tests and design rounds. Fix only what breaks the server the iPhone uses.
- New surfaces or features that aren't on this page.
- Design polish rounds on screens that work, unless the owner reports a bug.
- The marketing page is the marketing session's work (told on 16 Nov: iOS only, new wording; screenshots after week 3).

## Rules I follow every time (the owner's, collected)

- Think and research before any design; show it before building a new screen ("build it").
- Test by hand on the simulator with several cases before saying done; compare routes with Google and Apple.
- One pull request per change, merged only when CI is green; no Co-Authored-By line; never `--no-verify`.
- Releases to production and installs on the owner's iPhone only on his OK; tell him right after an install what changed.
- Haiku for every AI feature; ranking may use Sonnet, chosen by our own comparison. No paid batch runs (evals, grading) without his OK and a cost cap.
- No "AI" wording in the app except the legally required assistant notice (to be confirmed with the owner) and Settings.
- One start tap, Undo instead of confirmations, every action animates, swipes follow the finger, rounded rectangles (no pills).
- Personal data: opt-in per purpose, sensitive categories never learned, raw location stays on the phone, everything visible and deletable.
