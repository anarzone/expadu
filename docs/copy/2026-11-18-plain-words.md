# Plain, friendly words across the iPhone app (18 Nov 2026)

The owner, 18 Nov 2026: “We want to give user fully readable, friendly words.” The rule: whole everyday sentences, “you” and “your”, no technical words, nothing that needs a second read. Short buttons and compact rows (stop · minutes) stay short; prose and descriptions became full sentences. Meanings, promises and behaviour are unchanged.
