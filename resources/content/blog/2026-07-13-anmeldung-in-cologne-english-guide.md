The **Anmeldung** is your address registration, and almost everything else waits for it: your tax ID, a bank account, your residence permit appointment. Here is how it works in Cologne, from the city's own pages.

## The rule

You register **within two weeks of moving in**. The law says "innerhalb von zwei Wochen nach dem Einzug" ([§ 17 BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__17.html)), and the city's page says 14 days. The clock starts on the day you move into the flat, not the day you land in Germany.

Registering late is an administrative offence. The fine can be up to €1,000 ([§ 54 (2) no. 1 and (3) BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__54.html)). If no appointment is free in time, use the walk-in days below: that's the dependable way to make the deadline.

## Three ways to register

1. **Walk in on Monday or Wednesday.** Any of Cologne's nine Kundenzentren takes registrations without an appointment on Monday (7:30 to 15:00) and Wednesday (7:30 to 12:00). Expect a wait. The city: "Montags und mittwochs können Sie sich ohne Termin anmelden."
2. **Book an appointment** for Tuesday, Thursday or Friday. You can use any Kundenzentrum, wherever you live in the city. See the [Kundenzentren guide](/blog/kundenzentren-cologne-english-guide) for addresses and booking.
3. **Register online,** but only if you moved within Germany. The city's online service needs a BundID account and an ID card with its online function switched on. **If you are moving from abroad, you must go in person**, and you can't send someone else in your place: "Bei Zuzug aus dem Ausland müssen Sie immer selbst vorsprechen."

Registration is free.

## What to bring

- **Your passport** (the city says "Nationalpass"), plus any residence documents you already have, such as a visa.
- **The landlord form (Wohnungsgeberbescheinigung).** A rental contract or an informal letter is not enough, and you can't hand the form in later. The [landlord form guide](/blog/wohnungsgeberbescheinigung-cologne-english) explains what it must say.
- **For your family:** everyone's ID documents. Everyone over 16 signs their own form or gives a power of attorney. A single parent registering minor children brings the other parent's consent.
- **Moving from abroad with family:** your marriage or partnership certificate and the children's birth certificates, as originals. Documents not in German need a sworn translation.

## What you get afterwards

- **A free registration confirmation (Meldebestätigung),** given once with every registration. Keep it: banks and employers ask for it.
- **More certificates (Meldebescheinigung) cost €9 each.** Get them at the info desk of any Kundenzentrum, no appointment needed. They can't be requested online.
- **Registered online?** The address sticker for your ID card comes by post.
- **Your tax ID (Steuer-ID)** comes by post from the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern after your first registration. Don't throw away an official-looking letter. If nothing arrives within three months, the BZSt explains how to request it. It never gives the number out by phone or email.

## Moving again later

Moving within Cologne is an **Ummeldung**: same rules, same landlord form for the new flat, any Kundenzentrum. Moving abroad for good is the only time you deregister (Abmeldung).
