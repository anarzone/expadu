Looking for the **Bürgeramt** in Cologne? Here it's called a **Kundenzentrum**. There are nine, one in each district, and you can use any of them, wherever you live: "Sie können jedes Kundenzentrum besuchen, egal, wo Sie in Köln wohnen."

## Opening hours

The same hours apply at all nine:

| Day       | Hours         | Appointment? |
| --------- | ------------- | ------------ |
| Monday    | 7:30 to 15:00 | Not needed   |
| Tuesday   | 9:30 to 18:00 | Needed       |
| Wednesday | 7:30 to 12:00 | Not needed   |
| Thursday  | 7:30 to 16:00 | Needed       |
| Friday    | 7:30 to 12:00 | Needed       |

On the walk-in days the city warns you to expect a wait.

## What you can do there

- **Register your address** (Anmeldung, or Ummeldung when you move within Cologne). It's free: see the [Anmeldung guide](/blog/anmeldung-in-cologne-english-guide).
- **Get a registration certificate** (Meldebescheinigung): €9, at the info desk, no appointment.
- **Apply for an ID card** (Personalausweis) at any centre. You collect it at the same one.
- **Without an appointment, on any weekday,** the info desk handles a short list of things: collecting finished ID documents, applying for a certificate of good conduct (Führungszeugnis), registration and household certificates, switching on your ID card's online function, and a few more.
- **For anything else,** book an appointment or come on a Monday or Wednesday.

## Booking an appointment

1. Open the city's [online booking](https://termine.stadt-koeln.de/m/kundenzentren/extern/calendar/?uid=b5a5a394-ec33-4130-9af3-490f99517071) (category "Ausweise, Meldewesen und Sonstiges").
2. Choose your service and the number of people, then "Weiter", then a centre and a time.
3. Prefer the phone? Call [115](tel:115) or [0221 221-0](tel:+492212210).

The city doesn't say when new slots appear. Every centre's page says same-day appointments can be booked online or on [0221 221-0](tel:+492212210), so check on the morning you need one. If nothing fits your deadline, go on a Monday or Wednesday.

## Two recent changes

- **Passport photos:** printed photos are no longer accepted for ID cards and passports (since 1 May 2025). The centres have a self-service terminal that takes the digital photo for €6, paid with the document fee.
- **Driving licences:** the district centres' licence desks are shutting: Porz's closed on 12 October, Kalk's and Rodenkirchen's close on 26 October 2026, and Chorweiler's is already closed. Licence matters go to the Führerscheinstelle Innenstadt.
