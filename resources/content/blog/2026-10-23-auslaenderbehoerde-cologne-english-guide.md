Cologne's immigration office, the **Ausländeramt** (the law calls it the Ausländerbehörde), isn't one building. It's a central office in Kalk plus nine district offices. Which one you deal with depends on your permit and where you live. One rule covers all of them: **no walk-ins**. You need an appointment and an invitation letter from the office.

## Booking an appointment

- **Online, by postcode:** since 20 April 2026, students and people with humanitarian permits can book a district-office appointment themselves. A postcode lookup sends you to the right booking page: [book online](https://termine.stadt-koeln.de/m/Auslaenderamt/extern/calendar/?uid=a8035e3c-9559-4ac6-b328-59c3d5cc7113).
- **In writing, for everything else:** email, the city's [contact form](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/service/onlinedienste/kontakt-auslaenderamt/index.html), or post. You get a written invitation.
- **Bring the invitation** to the appointment, on your phone or printed.
- **Phone lines** at the district offices run Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 12:00 (numbers below).

## Extending your permit

**Apply before your current permit runs out.** If you do, your current permit counts as still valid until the office decides, and you get a certificate that says so, the Fiktionsbescheinigung ([§ 81 (4) AufenthG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__81.html), [city page](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/artikel/71447/index.html)).

The city offers an online extension application, after which it invites you in writing. Right now its [online services page](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/service/onlinedienste/auslaenderamt/index.html) says the online forms are unavailable for technical reasons. It offers PDF forms instead, and says applications already received take six weeks longer.

The city gives no general processing time; it depends on the permit and on whether your documents are complete. Fees it lists: Blue Card €100 for the first card and €93 for an extension; other extensions €93 to €96.
