You can't register your address in Cologne without one piece of paper from your landlord: the **Wohnungsgeberbescheinigung**, a written confirmation that you've moved in. Cologne calls it that. The law calls it the Bestätigung des Wohnungsgebers ([§ 19 BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__19.html)), and many websites say Wohnungsgeberbestätigung. It's the same form.

## Your rental contract doesn't count

The city is clear: "Der Mietvertrag oder ein formloses Bestätigungsschreiben reichen nicht aus." A contract or an informal letter isn't enough, and you can't hand the form in after your appointment. No form, no Anmeldung.

## What it must say

The law lists four things ([§ 19 (3) BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__19.html)):

1. The landlord's name and address and, if the landlord isn't the owner, the owner's name.
2. The date you moved in.
3. The address of the flat.
4. The names of everyone who is registering.

The city's form has fields for all of these. Download it from the city's [Anmeldung page](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/service/produkt/anmeldung-ihres-wohnsitzes).

## Who signs it, and by when

- **The person who gives you the flat** (the Wohnungsgeber), or someone they authorise. Nobody else may issue it ([§ 19 (1) BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__19.html)).
- **Subletting?** The person you rent from fills it in, and the owner's name must still appear (item 1 above).
- **Own your flat?** You fill in the self-declaration on the same form.
- **Timing:** your landlord must give it to you within two weeks of you moving in. The city: "Ihr\*e Vermieter\*in ist verpflichtet, Ihnen die Bescheinigung innerhalb von zwei Wochen … auszustellen."

Landlords can also confirm electronically, straight to the registration office, and then give you a code to use at your registration ([§ 19 (4) BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__19.html)). An informal email from your landlord to the office is not accepted.

## If your landlord won't give it to you

Tell the registration office **without delay**. That's your duty under the law: "so hat die meldepflichtige Person dies der Meldebehörde unverzüglich mitzuteilen" ([§ 19 (2) BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__19.html)). Go to a Kundenzentrum or contact it, and keep proof that you asked your landlord. Don't wait for the form and miss your own registration deadline.

The law takes this seriously on the landlord's side too: a landlord who doesn't confirm, or confirms wrongly or late, can be fined up to €1,000 ([§ 54 (2) no. 3 and (3) BMG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bmg/__54.html)).

## Before your appointment, check

- [ ] It's signed by your landlord (or the person you sublet from)
- [ ] The move-in date is the day you actually moved in
- [ ] Every name matches the passports exactly
- [ ] The owner's name is on it if your landlord isn't the owner

Then take it, with your passport, to your Anmeldung: see the [Anmeldung guide](/blog/anmeldung-in-cologne-english-guide).
